# Things to ask

Once Prepped is connected, talk to your coding tool the way you would to a colleague who has access to your files.

## Meetings

- "Set up a weekly lab meeting in Prepped, Fridays at 2 pm for an hour. I present the experiment results; the plan is in ./lab/plan.md and the results in ./results/summary.md. Answers in Chinese, short."
- "I have a 1:1 with my manager Thursday at 10. Make a Prepped meeting for it from the notes in ~/notes/1on1.md, and add the questions I'm likely to get about the Q3 launch."
- "Add this repository to next Friday's lab meeting so Prepped can look things up in the code during the meeting."
- "Move the design review to Monday at 3 pm and tell Prepped to keep answers under three sentences."

## Interviews

- "Create a Prepped interview for the backend role at Northwind on the 12th at 11. The posting is https://jobs.example.com/backend-123 and my resume is ./resume.pdf. Second round, technical."
- "Find ten system design questions in Prepped's question bank, save them, and start a practice run with the first five."

## Talks and defenses

- "My thesis defense is on the 20th at 10, 25 minutes. Slides are ./defense.pptx and my draft script is ./script.md. Set it up in Prepped and draft the outline. Ask me before anything that costs credits."

## Settings and reading back

- "Turn on Prepped's live translation into Chinese."
- "What did we decide in yesterday's lab meeting? Read the record from Prepped."
- "Show me everything the weekly lab meeting is set up with: time, material, projects and its shared memory."

## 中文也可以

- 「在 Prepped 上建立每周组会，每周三晚上八点，资料在 ./reports 里，回答要详细，翻译打开。」
- 「把上次组会的记录从 Prepped 读出来，整理成待办。」
